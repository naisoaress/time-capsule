Time Capsule started as a simple idea, inspired by the Pensieve from Harry Potter: a way to store and revisit memories on demand. Music does something similar, certain songs hold onto moments we might otherwise forget.
Whenever you want, you can "extract a memory": tell the program which song is tied to a moment, a feeling, something from your day. That memory gets sealed into a dated Word document, like a small record of the moment, and then deposited into the Time Capsule itself, a Spotify playlist that grows into the soundtrack of your life, one song at a time.
The long-term hope is that tools maybe could one day help people with memory loss reconnect with their memories through music. For now, it's a personal archive, one song, one memory, one day at a time.
